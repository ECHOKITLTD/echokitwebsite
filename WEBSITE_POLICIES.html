2. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your browser or device when you visit a website.

They can remember information about your visit, help a website operate or provide information about how the website is used.

Similar technologies, including tags and local storage, may perform related functions.

3. How we use cookies

Our website is informational and does not use ecommerce or advertising cookies.

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand, in aggregate, how visitors find and use the website.

Google Analytics may help us measure:

  • The number of visitors to the website;
  • The pages visitors view;
  • How visitors arrived at the website;
  • Approximate visitor location;
  • Device and browser types; and
  • Interactions with website content.

Analytics is not necessary for the website to work. We therefore ask for your consent before enabling Google Analytics and its cookies.

Refusing analytics cookies will not prevent you from using the website.

Our cookie-consent tool may use a strictly necessary cookie or local-storage entry to remember your preference. This storage is used only to provide the privacy choice you requested and is not used for analytics or advertising.

4. Google Analytics cookies

If you consent, Google Analytics 4 may set the following first-party cookies:

_ga

Purpose: Used to distinguish one visitor from another and support visitor and session statistics.
Default duration: Up to two years.

_ga_PXEF3K112K
Provider: Google Analytics
Purpose: Used to maintain session information for the website’s Google Analytics property.
Default duration: Up to two years.

The actual lifetime of these cookies may be shorter because of browser restrictions, deletion by the user or changes to our configuration.

Google Analytics may change the names or operation of its cookies. We will review this policy if our analytics setup changes.

5. Mailing-list form

The website’s mailing-list form is provided by Mailchimp.

The form sends the details you choose to enter to Mailchimp when you submit it. The form embedded on our website is not intended to place Mailchimp marketing cookies on your device before submission.

Mailchimp may use cookies under its own cookie policy when a Mailchimp page opens during the subscription process or when you interact with an email.

Mailchimp’s cookie information is available at:
https://www.intuit.com/privacy/statement/cookies/

6. Your cookie choices

When you first visit the website, our cookie controls should allow you to accept or reject analytics cookies.

Google Analytics should remain disabled unless and until you make a clear choice to accept it.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time by selecting “Cookie settings” on the website.

Withdrawing consent does not delete information already collected, but it prevents further analytics storage after your updated preference is applied.

You can also delete or block cookies using your browser settings. Blocking all cookies may affect preference controls on some websites.

Google provides a browser add-on for opting out of Google Analytics across participating websites:
https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

7. Further information

For information about how we process personal information associated with website analytics, please read our Privacy Policy.
