BARK

Acoustic Wall Tiles Inspired by Nature

BARK is a collection of acoustic wall tiles that help create calmer, clearer spaces through sound control and sculptural surface design. Inspired by the textures and contours of native UK trees, BARK introduces depth, rhythm and acoustic comfort to commercial interiors, workplaces and shared spaces.

Designed and manufactured in the UK, BARK brings together acoustic performance, architectural texture and responsible material choices in a single modular system.

Designed to Bring Texture and Acoustic Comfort Together

The way a space sounds can be just as important as the way it looks. Noise and hard surfaces can create distracting environments that are difficult to concentrate in and uncomfortable to occupy.

Inspired by the textures found in nature, BARK introduces depth and rhythm to interior surfaces while helping to reduce reverberation and soften the acoustic environment. The result is a calmer, more comfortable space that supports focus, wellbeing and everyday activity.

Available in a range of designs and PET felt colours, BARK can be used to create subtle acoustic treatments or bold feature walls.

SYCAMORE

Flowing vertical forms that bring rhythm and texture to interior walls.

MAPLE

Linear textures that create a clean and structured surface.

CONKER

Stepped forms that create depth and movement. Its natural form helps create a more comfortable environment by combining sound absorption with a warm, textural finish.

WILLOW

Bold profiles that create depth and definition.. Its acoustic felt form helps absorb sound while bringing a lighter, more natural texture into the space.

BEAM

Clean lines with a refined architectural character. It supports acoustic control while offering a more architectural expression within the BARK collection.

LARCH

Deep relief that creates shadow and depth.

Better Acoustics. Better Spaces

Sound shapes how we experience a space. By reducing unwanted noise and introducing natural texture, BARK helps create environments that feel calmer, clearer and more comfortable for everyone.

Made with Purpose.

Good design works for both people and the environment.

BARK is designed and manufactured in the UK using low-impact materials and a thoughtful approach to longevity and reuse. Each panel is made from recycled PET felt, transforming post-consumer plastic waste into an acoustic material designed to perform for years.
