SOUNDART

Acoustic Wall Art Inspired by Nature

SOUNDART is a collection of acoustic wall art that combines sound absorption with nature-inspired design. Each piece brings colour, texture and visual interest to interior spaces while helping to create a calmer acoustic environment.

Inspired by botanical forms and natural patterns, SOUNDART offers a creative way to manage noise without compromising aesthetics. The result is a space that feels calmer, more comfortable and more engaging to spend time in.

KLOVER

Combines geometric forms with the organic structure of clover leaves, creating a balance between order and natural variation.

BULRUSH

Based on the vertical forms and rhythm of waterside grasses, creating a bold and structured visual language.

LILYPAD

Draws on the simple, circular forms of lily pads to introduce softness and balance to interior spaces.

WALLFLOWER

Captures the layered forms of flowering plants, creating acoustic features with depth and texture.

LOTUS

Echoing the distinctive petal structure of lotus flowers, LOTUS combines sculptural depth with acoustic performance.

TWINFLOWER

Reflects the symmetry found in nature, creating visually distinctive acoustic installations with a balanced and structured appearance.

Made with Purpose

Good design works for both people and the environment.

SOUNDART is designed and manufactured in the UK using recycled PET felt and low-impact production methods. Each panel transforms post-consumer plastic waste into an acoustic material designed to perform for years.
