TRELLIS

Defining Space, Softening Sound

TRELLIS is a collection of acoustic screens that divide space, manage noise and create greater privacy within open environments.

Made from recycled PET felt, TRELLIS shapes workplaces, education settings and shared interiors through a range of graphic and organic designs that balance acoustic performance with visual character.

Create Zones Without Closing Spaces Off

pen environments support collaboration, but they can create distractions, reduce privacy and make it difficult to concentrate.

TRELLIS helps define areas within a space while maintaining openness and flexibility. By combining acoustic performance with thoughtful design, it creates quieter, more comfortable environments that support focus, communication and everyday activity.

Suitable for workplaces, education settings, hospitality venues and shared interiors, TRELLIS offers a simple way to introduce both acoustic separation and visual structure.

FLORA

Evenly spaced perforations create a clean and restrained design suited to subtle acoustic separation.

FLORA LARGE

Inspired by pixel-like patterns, combining precise geometry with a contemporary character.

ASTER

Large circular perforations arranged in a confident grid, creating a bold and graphic acoustic divider.

BALE

Vertical slots arranged in a disciplined grid, introducing rhythm and structure to shared spaces.

BATCH

Staggered slot lengths create movement across the panel while maintaining acoustic performance.

KADO

Organic cut-outs introduce softness and visual texture while helping define different areas within a space.

KIRIN

A denser floral-inspired pattern that balances acoustic screening with visual character.

Made with Purpose

Good design works for both people and the environment.

TRELLIS is designed and manufactured in the UK using recycled PET felt and low-impact production methods. Each screen transforms post-consumer plastic waste into an acoustic material designed to perform for years.
